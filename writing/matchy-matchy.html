Matchy Matchy: matching customer feedback to issues you already know about

Most incoming feedback isn't new. The hard part is noticing that fast enough to act on what actually is.

Every feedback program hits the same wall. Customers describe one problem in a hundred different ways, and each report lands as if it were new. Triage teams spend their time asking "haven't we seen this already?" instead of routing what matters.

I built Matchy Matchy to answer that question automatically. It takes a new piece of customer feedback and finds the existing issues that mean the same thing, even when they share almost no words.

How it works

Matchy Matchy compares feedback by meaning instead of by keywords:

  • Azure AI Search holds the index of existing issues and runs the similarity search.
  • GitHub Models / Azure OpenAI provide the language models behind the matching.
  • MCP (Model Context Protocol) connects the matcher to the AI tools in the triage workflow.

Why it matters

When duplicates collapse into the issue they belong to, two things get better. Volume becomes a signal: ten reports on one issue tell you more than ten separate tickets. And the feedback that doesn't match anything stands out as the new problem worth a closer look.

Matchy Matchy grew out of my work on feedback systems at GitHub, where routing the right signal to the right team quickly was the whole job.
